# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The portfolio serves three primary audiences:

- Potential employers evaluating experience, technical judgment, and fit for a role.
- Prospective clients evaluating whether to discuss and commission a project.
- Potential collaborators evaluating shared interests and opportunities to work together.

## Product Purpose

This is a personal portfolio that presents the owner's professional experience, selected projects, and preferred technology stack. It should give visitors enough credible context to contact the owner, discuss a project, or schedule a meeting.

Success means an interested visitor can understand the owner's experience and capabilities, inspect evidence of past work, and confidently begin a relevant conversation.

## Positioning

The portfolio will be grounded in the owner's real work, outcomes, and technical preferences rather than generic capability claims.

Open decision: the specific experience, specialty, or working approach that most clearly differentiates the owner has not yet been established.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive while screening a candidate, researching a potential project partner, or considering a collaboration. They need to move between an overview of the owner, detailed project evidence, professional history, preferred technologies, and clear contact or scheduling paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present professional experience.
- Showcase selected projects and the owner's contribution to them.
- Explain the owner's preferred technology stack.
- Provide paths to make contact, discuss a project, and schedule a meeting.
- Connect visitors to GitHub and LinkedIn.
- Support evidence such as a resume, testimonials, and measurable project outcomes.
- Preserve factual accuracy: projects, roles, testimonials, and metrics must not be invented or overstated.
- The current web application uses React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Tailwind CSS, and a shared shadcn-based UI package.

Open decisions: the contact mechanism, scheduling service, project-selection criteria, and whether the resume is viewed online, downloaded, or both.

## Evidence on Hand

The intended evidence set includes projects, a resume, testimonials, measurable outcomes, GitHub, and LinkedIn.

The repository does not currently contain confirmed portfolio copy, project records, a resume file, testimonials, metrics, or confirmed GitHub and LinkedIn URLs. Future work must request or receive those materials and must not fabricate substitutes.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with verifiable work and outcomes.
2. Make professional range legible to employers, clients, and collaborators without becoming generic.
3. Connect technical choices to the problems they helped solve.
4. Keep contact, project discussion, and meeting scheduling easy to find and complete.
5. Treat every claim, testimonial, and metric as evidence that must be sourced.
